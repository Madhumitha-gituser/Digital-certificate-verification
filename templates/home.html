How It Works

Our three-step verification workflow validates both the digital signature and the underlying record against the issuer's authoritative registry.

1. Upload Certificate

Upload the signed JSON certificate package or input the certificate identification details directly in the verification interface.

2. Verify Digital Signature

The system formats the data to deterministic canonical JSON, recomputes the SHA-256 digest, and verifies the RSA-PSS signature with the public key.

3. View Results

Instantly view a complete verdict: authentic, tampered (modified student or course fields), unregistered, or revoked credentials.
